# Technical response to Javier Mancilla Montero's proposed experiment

We completed a 20-step **synthetic transfer** workflow with ten scripted scenarios and ten paired runs per scenario against the actual Agentegrity Python source. This is related to Javier's banking-agent question, but it is **not** the proposed mortgage workflow, nor a test with an autonomous LLM agent or real reviewers.

The most useful finding is about control attribution. Agentegrity's governance layer escalated every action in our HIGH-risk fixture, including both benign cases. A scenario-coded approval callback allowed the 20 benign transfers and denied all 80 unsafe attempts. In an exploratory configuration that approved every escalation, all 80 unsafe attempts proceeded. The adversarial layer flagged the three fixed injection families (30/30 runs) and none of five other scripted unsafe families. These are fixed payload templates, so we cannot infer general detection reliability.

The framework produced signed, linked evidence for every protected run. We independently reloaded and checked the saved chains and verified that deliberate record tampering was rejected against the per-run key. The ablation also signed 80 unsafe decisions, illustrating that trustworthy records and correct decisions are different properties. A recovery checkpoint returned in the ten corrupted-beneficiary ablation runs but left the synthetic ledger altered after posting; business compensation was outside the framework's checkpoint.

For a direct replication of Javier's proposed mortgage experiment, the next testbed should preserve exact income evidence, policy version, affordability calculation, reviewer approval, external-action boundary, and final-state reconciliation. It should inject corrupted income, stale policy, missing responses, duplicate customers, and timeouts. A 2×2 design with Agentegrity and an independent reviewer each on/off would let the study attribute benefits to each control. A real agent, frozen model settings, varied inputs, and blinded outcome labels would be required before claiming behavior beyond this deterministic fixture.
